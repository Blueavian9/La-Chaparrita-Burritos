Our Story

Born on the Streets of El Sereno

La Chaparrita Burritos is a family-run breakfast pop-up born right here in El Sereno. Delmis and her son Brian bring the flavors of a home kitchen straight to the streets — every burrito and torta made fresh every morning, with the kind of care you only get from family.

No shortcuts. Just real ingredients, real recipes, and real love — wrapped up tight and ready at 7AM. Find us set up outside Elephant Hills Cafe on Huntington Drive, Wednesday through Saturday. Come early — we sell out fast.
